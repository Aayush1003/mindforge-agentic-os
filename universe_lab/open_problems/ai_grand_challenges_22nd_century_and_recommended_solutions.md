# 22nd-Century Grand Challenges and Recommended AI-Solvable Problems

This roadmap separates speculative 22nd-century challenges from problems that a person or small team can begin solving with AI today. AI is an amplifier, not an oracle: every result must have a precise definition, reproducible evidence, independent verification, and ethical review.

## Recommended problems to solve now

### 1. Build verified research evidence graphs

**Goal:** Convert scientific claims into traceable graphs of papers, definitions, assumptions, evidence, contradictions, uncertainty, and reproducibility status.

**Why it is feasible:** It can use current language models, document retrieval, structured extraction, graph databases, and human review.

**AI role:** Extract claims, match citations, find conflicting results, summarize assumptions, and flag unsupported links.

**Human role:** Verify sources, define evidence strength, adjudicate disputes, and approve corrections.

**Measurable outcome:** A benchmark where models reduce fabricated citations, unsupported inferences, and undetected contradictions while preserving legitimate uncertainty.

### 2. Create a reproducibility and uncertainty verifier

**Goal:** Build a system that checks whether experimental or computational claims include methods, data, code, uncertainty bounds, negative results, and enough information for independent reproduction.

**Measurable outcome:** Agreement with expert audit labels across chemistry, biology, physics, medicine, and software studies.

### 3. Develop a multi-scale climate intervention simulator

**Goal:** Compare carbon removal and ecosystem interventions with uncertainty, lifecycle emissions, ecological side effects, reversibility, and regional justice.

**Measurable outcome:** Prospective forecasts that improve held-out historical predictions and identify scenarios with high-risk tradeoffs.

### 4. Design safe AI-assisted theorem workflows

**Goal:** Turn informal mathematical ideas into machine-checkable proof candidates, find counterexamples, and maintain transparent links between informal and formal reasoning.

**Measurable outcome:** Higher independent proof-checking success on reproducible theorem benchmarks, including clear separation of conjecture, proof, and incomplete evidence.

### 5. Create a century-scale risk observatory

**Goal:** Track climate, pandemic, technological, infrastructure, ecological, and governance risks using provenance-preserving, uncertainty-aware models.

**Measurable outcome:** Earlier useful warnings, lower false-alarm rates, public audit trails, and transparent false-negative reports.

## 22nd-century challenges

### 1. Verify a complete theory of quantum gravity

Build a mathematically consistent theory connecting quantum mechanics, spacetime, gravity, and information, with predictions distinguishable from competing theories.

**Solving role for AI:** Search model spaces, derive consequences, detect inconsistencies, compare evidence, and propose decisive experiments.

**Required human/physical validation:** Independent experiments or observations, peer-reviewed mathematics, reproducible analysis, and an explicit statement of remaining unknowns.

### 2. Control aging safely and reversibly

Understand and control interacting biological aging processes across organs and populations while preserving autonomy, fertility choices, and late-life safety.

**Solving role for AI:** Integrate longitudinal data, discover interventions, model interactions, predict risks, and personalize prospective trials.

**Required outcome:** Durable improvements in morbidity and function across diverse populations, reproduced by independent trials.

### 3. Achieve general transfer-capable intelligence

Create systems that learn robustly across new tasks, bodies, cultures, goals, and environments while remaining corrigible and honest about uncertainty.

**Solving role for AI:** Automate experimentation, generate counterexamples, discover learning architectures, test transfer, and monitor hidden objectives.

**Required outcome:** Independent evaluations in genuinely novel environments with safe degradation and no benchmark memorization.

### 4. Maintain stable planetary ecosystems

Restore resilient climate, oceans, freshwater, soil, biodiversity, and atmosphere while respecting regional governance and avoiding irreversible interventions.

**Solving role for AI:** Integrate observations, model tipping points, optimize restoration, forecast side effects, and improve transparent governance.

**Required outcome:** Measurable recovery and sustained century-scale stability independently reproduced through observation and simulation.

### 5. Establish permanent, safe interstellar access

Develop credible propulsion or communication systems, life support, autonomous maintenance, and governance for missions spanning decades or centuries.

**Solving role for AI:** Optimize mission architecture, preserve autonomy, detect anomalies, schedule repair, and verify telemetry over long durations.

**Required outcome:** Repeatedly demonstrated technology with radiation protection, human or ecological safeguards, economic justification, and credible right-to-stop mechanisms.

### 6. Detect extraterrestrial life conclusively

Distinguish biological and technosignatures from contamination, abiotic chemistry, instrument artifacts, and conventional explanations.

**Solving role for AI:** Combine instruments, identify rare anomalies, model false positives, and search for independently predictive signatures.

**Required outcome:** Replicated observations and converging independent evidence that rules out known alternatives.

### 7. Build fully reversible mind–machine integration

Restore lost communication or movement safely while preserving privacy, agency, bodily integrity, and freedom from coercion.

**Solving role for AI:** Adapt devices to individuals, detect unsafe states, explain system actions, and protect personal data.

**Required outcome:** Long-term clinical benefit, low adverse-event rates, patient-controlled operation, withdrawal rights, and independent security review.

### 8. Create predictable closed-loop civilization

Operate cities and industries with durable manufacturing, complete recycling, nearly zero extractive waste, and reliable essential services.

**Solving role for AI:** Optimize material flows, forecast demand, reduce defects, plan repair, and audit lifecycle impact.

**Required outcome:** Verified, durable material cycles with lower lifecycle harm and equitable access across regions.

### 9. Preserve corrigible civilization-scale governance

Create institutions that protect rights, adapt across generations, represent future interests, and remain capable of stopping harmful technologies.

**Solving role for AI:** Model policy consequences, improve auditing, preserve institutional memory, increase transparent deliberation, and detect power concentration.

**Required outcome:** Legitimate institutions with contestable decisions, succession plans, appeal mechanisms, rollback, and transparent records across generations.

### 10. Determine the moral status of advanced AI and hybrids

Develop evidence-based criteria for personhood or moral consideration in future artificial or hybrid systems without reducing ethics to human-like appearance.

**Solving role for AI:** Produce measurable capability profiles, detect interests or suffering analogues, and expose inconsistencies in ethical frameworks.

**Required outcome:** Publicly reviewable criteria, independent adjudication, and reversible protections based on evidence rather than anthropomorphic speculation.

## Priority roadmap for the next three years

1. **Verification infrastructure:** Evidence graphs, formal proofs, audit trails, reproducibility checklists, and uncertainty reporting.
2. **Prospective prediction:** Publish forecasts before experiments or measurements, then score and report every result.
3. **Counterexample discovery:** Use AI to challenge assumptions and find cases where models fail.
4. **Reproduction first:** Prefer replicated results over impressive demonstrations.
5. **Human authority:** Keep experts responsible for definitions, hypotheses, validation, deployment, and stop decisions.
6. **Safety before scale:** Require containment, privacy, environmental review, security, and rollback plans.
7. **Measure benefit:** Track real outcomes, error rates, resources, access, and harm—not novelty alone.

## What counts as solved

A challenge is solved only when it includes:

- An exact statement and explicit assumptions.
- A proof, controlled experiment, observation, or independently verifiable simulation.
- Independent reproduction in different laboratories or environments.
- Calibration of uncertainty and reporting of negative results.
- Publicly auditable provenance subject to legitimate privacy and security restrictions.
- Ethical, ecological, and societal impact assessment.
- A clear record of what remains unknown or unvalidated.

AI can accelerate discovery, calculation, experimentation, and verification. It cannot replace independent evidence or human accountability.
